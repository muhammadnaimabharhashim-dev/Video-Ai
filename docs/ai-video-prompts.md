# Pek skrip video AI: Kucing Kejar Anjing

Prompt dalam bahasa Inggeris kerana kebanyakan alat video AI memberi hasil terbaik dengan bahasa itu. Jana setiap syot satu per satu, kemudian sambung.

## Cara guna

1. Mula dengan 'image-to-video': jana dulu satu gambar kucing dan satu gambar anjing yang cantik, kemudian guna gambar yang sama sebagai rujukan untuk setiap klip supaya rupa mereka konsisten.
2. Salin blok 'watak' (kucing dan anjing) dengan sama persis dalam setiap prompt. Jangan ubah perkataan, kerana itu yang menjaga warna bulu dan saiz.
3. Jana 2 hingga 4 versi setiap syot, pilih yang terbaik. Klip 3 hingga 8 saat paling stabil; klip panjang mudah rosak.
4. Gunakan prompt negatif (kalau alat itu ada) untuk mengelakkan rupa kartun dan kaki yang cacat.
5. Sambung klip di CapCut atau DaVinci Resolve. Letak bunyi akhir (bunyi persekitaran dan muzik) di situ jika alat anda tiada audio sendiri.
6. Alat video AI yang popular: Google Veo, OpenAI Sora, Kling, Runway, Luma, Hailuo. Ciri dan harga berubah dengan cepat, jadi semak dulu apa yang ada pada akaun anda.

## Blok watak dan gaya (ulang dalam setiap prompt)

- Kucing: an orange tabby cat with a white chest and white paws, green eyes, short dense fur, slightly chubby, about 4 kg
- Anjing: a medium-sized golden-tan village dog with floppy ears, a dark muzzle and an athletic build, about 18 kg
- Latar: a rustic Southeast Asian kampung lane at golden hour: dirt road, weathered wooden fences, tall grass, palm trees, an old wooden porch
- Gaya: cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden-hour backlight, natural motion blur, subtle film grain, 24 fps, 16:9
- Prompt negatif: cartoon, 3D render look, text, subtitles, watermark, extra limbs, deformed paws, morphing animals, changing fur colour, cut mid-motion

## Senarai syot

### Syot 1: Beranda: anjing curi ikan (6 s)
Kamera: Slow dolly-in, low angle, shallow focus on the cat

**Prompt**: a rustic Southeast Asian kampung lane at golden hour: dirt road, weathered wooden fences, tall grass, palm trees, an old wooden porch. an orange tabby cat with a white chest and white paws, green eyes, short dense fur, slightly chubby, about 4 kg sleeps curled on a woven mat on the porch, breathing gently, ear twitching. Beside it a white ceramic bowl holds a fresh silver fish. a medium-sized golden-tan village dog with floppy ears, a dark muzzle and an athletic build, about 18 kg tiptoes in from the right, lowers its head, quietly lifts the fish in its mouth and backs away, glancing at the cat. Slow dolly-in, low camera, dust motes in the sunlight. cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden-hour backlight, natural motion blur, subtle film grain, 24 fps, 16:9.

**Bunyi**: Cicadas, soft wind, faint cat purr, tiny wooden creaks of paws on the porch

### Syot 2: Mata kucing terbuka (3 s)
Kamera: Extreme close-up, rack focus

**Prompt**: Extreme close-up of an orange tabby cat with a white chest and white paws, green eyes, short dense fur, slightly chubby, about 4 kg: the eyelid snaps open, green eye with a slit pupil that contracts sharply, fur rim-lit by the setting sun, a blurred dog silhouette running away in the background. Rack focus from the whiskers to the eye. cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden-hour backlight, natural motion blur, subtle film grain, 24 fps, 16:9.

**Bunyi**: Low heartbeat, a rising swell, a short angry hiss

### Syot 3: Pengejaran: sisi (5 s)
Kamera: Handheld tracking shot at dog height

**Prompt**: a rustic Southeast Asian kampung lane at golden hour: dirt road, weathered wooden fences, tall grass, palm trees, an old wooden porch. Side tracking shot at ground level: a medium-sized golden-tan village dog with floppy ears, a dark muzzle and an athletic build, about 18 kg sprints along the dirt lane with a silver fish in its mouth, ears flapping, dust kicking up behind each paw; an orange tabby cat with a white chest and white paws, green eyes, short dense fur, slightly chubby, about 4 kg bursts after it a few metres behind, fur streaked with speed. Wooden fence posts flicker through the foreground, blurred tall grass in the foreground. cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden-hour backlight, natural motion blur, subtle film grain, 24 fps, 16:9.

**Bunyi**: Rapid paw thuds on dirt, heavy panting, a driving drum rhythm

### Syot 4: Pengejaran: depan (5 s)
Kamera: Low front-facing angle, camera retreating

**Prompt**: Low front-facing camera retreating at running speed: a medium-sized golden-tan village dog with floppy ears, a dark muzzle and an athletic build, about 18 kg runs straight toward the lens looking panicked, the fish swinging from its mouth, an orange tabby cat with a white chest and white paws, green eyes, short dense fur, slightly chubby, about 4 kg furious a few metres behind with narrowed eyes and flattened ears. Long dirt lane with fences converging to a wooden gate in the background, dust haze glowing in the backlight. cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden-hour backlight, natural motion blur, subtle film grain, 24 fps, 16:9.

**Bunyi**: Pounding paws, wind rush, dog whimper

### Syot 5: Jerami meletup (5 s)
Kamera: Static wide, slow motion

**Prompt**: Wide static shot, slow motion: a medium-sized golden-tan village dog with floppy ears, a dark muzzle and an athletic build, about 18 kg leaps gracefully over a round hay bale in the middle of the lane. A moment later an orange tabby cat with a white chest and white paws, green eyes, short dense fur, slightly chubby, about 4 kg misjudges the jump and smashes straight into the bale, which explodes into golden straw filling the air. cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden-hour backlight, natural motion blur, subtle film grain, 24 fps, 16:9, 120 fps slow motion.

**Bunyi**: Deep thump, a crunchy straw explosion, a comic cat yowl

### Syot 6: Kucing berjerami (3 s)
Kamera: Tracking shot, medium

**Prompt**: Tracking shot: an orange tabby cat with a white chest and white paws, green eyes, short dense fur, slightly chubby, about 4 kg emerges from the straw cloud with pieces of straw stuck all over its fur and one piece on its head, still running with grim determination, spitting out straw. cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden-hour backlight, natural motion blur, subtle film grain, 24 fps, 16:9.

**Bunyi**: Paw thuds, a comic sneeze, wind

### Syot 7: Lopak lumpur (5 s)
Kamera: Ground-level, backlit, slow motion

**Prompt**: Ground-level camera facing the setting sun beside a wide muddy puddle: a medium-sized golden-tan village dog with floppy ears, a dark muzzle and an athletic build, about 18 kg jumps over the puddle in a huge leap, an orange tabby cat with a white chest and white paws, green eyes, short dense fur, slightly chubby, about 4 kg hits the water and hydroplanes on its belly, spinning in a full circle with a backlit spray of glittering droplets, then shakes off the water. cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden-hour backlight, natural motion blur, subtle film grain, 24 fps, 16:9, slow motion.

**Bunyi**: A big splash, a long slide hiss, falling droplets, a cat shake

### Syot 8: Pintu pagar: kucing menang (8 s)
Kamera: Slow push-in, then a low two-shot

**Prompt**: a medium-sized golden-tan village dog with floppy ears, a dark muzzle and an athletic build, about 18 kg is cornered against a closed wooden farm gate at the end of the lane, ears flat, trembling. an orange tabby cat with a white chest and white paws, green eyes, short dense fur, slightly chubby, about 4 kg, wet and fluffed up with its tail high, stalks closer and stares it down. The dog sadly drops the fish on the ground and sits. The cat sniffs the fish, picks it up gently in its mouth and walks away down the lane while the dog lies down with a long sigh. Slow push-in, then a low two-shot. cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden-hour backlight, natural motion blur, subtle film grain, 24 fps, 16:9.

**Bunyi**: A tense low drone, a dog whimper, a fish plop, a soft cat growl, then a warm gentle melody
